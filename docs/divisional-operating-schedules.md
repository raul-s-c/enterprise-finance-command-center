# Divisional Operating Schedules

Version 0.5 adds business-model-specific operating schedules behind the consolidated finance model.

The objective is not to create more dashboard metrics. It is to expose the economic drivers that a Finance Director would use to understand why revenue, margin, Working Capital and future cash move differently across the four divisions.

## Design rule

The four divisions deliberately do not share one generic KPI framework.

```text
Software     -> recurring revenue and retention
Events       -> bookings, backlog and revenue conversion
Hardware     -> capacity, production mix and fixed-cost absorption
Spare Parts  -> installed base, aftermarket monetization and stock coverage
```

Where a schedule can be reconciled directly to operating data, the release contains a hard validation control. Where a schedule is an analytical extension, the calculation is deterministic and traceable to the same operating dataset.

## Software

The Software schedule decomposes monthly customer-product revenue into recurring and service components.

Family-level recurring assumptions reflect different commercial models:

- Platform: 92% recurring
- Security: 90% recurring
- Analytics: 84% recurring
- Automation: 82% recurring

For each customer-product position the engine calculates:

```text
Opening MRR
+ New MRR
+ Expansion MRR
- Contraction MRR
- Churn MRR
= Ending MRR
```

Annualized values produce ARR, New ARR, Expansion ARR, Contraction ARR and Churn ARR.

The management layer also calculates:

- Net Revenue Retention (NRR)
- Gross Revenue Retention (GRR)
- recurring revenue mix
- services revenue
- ARR by legal entity
- largest customer-product recurring positions

The complete Software schedule must reconcile to the Software revenue generated by the operating model.

## Events & Projects

Events is modeled as a project business rather than recurring revenue.

For each legal entity and project family:

```text
Opening Backlog
+ Bookings
- Recognized Revenue
= Ending Backlog
```

The schedule derives deterministic bookings from recognized activity, seasonality and commercial momentum. It calculates:

- bookings
- recognized revenue
- ending backlog
- book-to-bill
- backlog coverage in months
- project units

The backlog roll-forward is a hard validation control.

## Hardware

Hardware economics start with the two manufacturing sites:

- Brno Smart Manufacturing (CZ01)
- Suzhou Manufacturing Hub (CN01)

For each factory and month the schedule calculates:

- produced units
- available capacity
- utilization
- capacity headroom
- fixed factory cost
- absorbed fixed cost
- under-absorption
- fixed cost per produced unit

The product mix schedule adds:

- product family
- quality tier
- units
- external revenue
- external gross profit
- unit mix percentage
- external gross margin

Factory utilization is recalculated independently from produced units and capacity and compared to the accounting-engine factory output.

### Accounting integration roadmap

Version 0.5 treats fixed-cost absorption as a management schedule. A future release should post explicit absorbed and under-absorbed production cost so the factory-capacity schedule also becomes a direct P&L bridge instead of only an operational explanation.

## Spare Parts

Aftermarket demand is linked to the historical installed base created by Hardware sales.

For each Spare Parts legal entity:

```text
Opening Installed Base
+ Hardware Additions
- Estimated Retirements
= Ending Installed Base
```

A deterministic monthly survival factor models retirement of the installed base.

The schedule then calculates:

- installed base
- hardware additions
- estimated retirements
- Spare Parts revenue
- Spare Parts units
- active SKUs
- revenue per installed unit
- inventory value
- inventory coverage months
- inventory health

Inventory health uses the reconciled inventory-aging schedule introduced in v0.4, so slow-moving and obsolescence risk directly influence the aftermarket management view.

## Dashboard

Version 0.5 adds a dedicated `Business Drivers` view.

The global Division filter changes the view to the relevant business-model schedule rather than forcing unrelated KPIs into one table.

The view contains:

- Software ARR and retention economics
- Events bookings and backlog
- Hardware factory economics and production mix
- Spare Parts installed-base and aftermarket economics

## Release controls

The v0.5 build fails if any of the following exceed tolerance:

- Software revenue schedule vs operating revenue
- Software ARR roll-forward
- Events backlog roll-forward
- factory utilization recalculation
- Spare Parts installed-base roll-forward

These controls are added to the existing accounting, consolidation, Working Capital and forecast validation suite.

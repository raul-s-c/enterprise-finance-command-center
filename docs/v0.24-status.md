# v0.24 Status — Source-accrual payables evidence

Version 0.24 extends the external AP schedule from supplier/month to source
accrual journal ID while preserving the published GL, cash flow and supplier
aging balances.

## Reporting contract

- A source item ID is the AP accrual journal ID. It is not represented as a
  supplier's commercial invoice number.
- Aggregate AP reductions are allocated under the existing supplier aging
  policy: factory absorption variance first; prefer the posted division and
  fall back to the legal entity; apply to the oldest accrual, then supplier
  criticality and stable source ID.
- Every modeled application preserves the reduction journal ID, month, type,
  amount and basis. It is not a vendor remittance match.
- Open source-item balances reconcile by entity, division, supplier and aging
  bucket to the published supplier AP schedule. Modeled applications reconcile
  to their source AP reduction entries. No GL or cash entry is created.
- The AP contribution page only offers this detail for the current published
  close; earlier periods remain available in the supplier aging without a
  misleading blank item-level view.

## Published evidence and experience

The build writes `data/processed/ap_item_aging.csv`,
`data/processed/ap_item_applications.csv` and the lazy-loaded
`web/data/ap_item_detail.json`. The Payables contribution page exposes a
searchable, paginated mobile-friendly detail view and a source trace with
modeled reduction history. Receivables likewise hides invoice evidence when a
historical period is selected because the detail artifact represents the
current close only.

## Verification

- Tests reconcile generated source-accrual detail against supplier aging,
  reconcile reductions to source journal IDs, reject missing identifiers and
  over-application, and preserve the input journal.
- The close continuity regression runs two successive offline v0.24 builds,
  preserves action/review snapshots and validates AR, AP and all 24 forecast
  periods.
- Mobile UI regressions inspect invoice and AP source-accrual traces, check
  modeled-allocation disclosures and assert no horizontal overflow.

## Evidence boundary

Source AP accruals can represent cost accruals rather than vendor invoices.
Payment IDs are aggregate entity/division postings. Real supplier invoice
numbers, remittance advice, bank references and actual invoice-to-payment
matching are not in the source model. Inventory remains SKU-grain rather than
warehouse-lot grain; provisions and consolidation adjustments are not assigned
to individual supplier items.

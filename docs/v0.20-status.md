# v0.20 Release Status

Transaction FX Exposure and Remeasurement release.

Release scope:

- source-journal-tied AR, AP and intercompany foreign-currency documents
- deterministic contract currency and payment terms
- functional-currency monthly remeasurement
- separate realized and unrealized FX P&L
- document lifecycle, ageing, currency exposure and Group summary
- explicit separation from CTA and historical-statement postings
- duplicate, currency, carrying-value, lifecycle and summary controls
- filtered `FX & Translation` dashboard view
- deterministic offline compatibility

The standard August 2026 offline close produces 42,978 transaction documents, 105,126 monthly snapshots and 830 summary rows. At the current close, 1,769 documents remain open with EUR 399,753.72 net exposure, EUR -42,712.50 unrealized FX P&L and EUR 39,607.99 realized FX P&L.

Version 0.20 retains every accounting, consolidation, Working Capital, Treasury, Budget, forecast, workforce, FX translation, performance-review, action-lifecycle, benefits-realization and macro-sensitivity control introduced through v0.19.

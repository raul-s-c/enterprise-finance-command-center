# v0.29.13 status

## Cash flow to closing cash

The Cash Flow cockpit now teaches the published group cash bridge in three linked equations:

```text
Operating cash flow + Investing cash flow = Free cash flow
Free cash flow + Financing cash flow = Net cash movement
Opening cash + Net cash movement = Closing Balance Sheet cash
```

Opening cash comes from the immediately preceding published month; closing cash comes from the current close. The cockpit compares those source values and reports the €0.05 reconciliation tolerance. A difference above tolerance is labelled for review. The tie-out reads `cash_flow` and `balance_sheet`; it does not create a posting, balancing item or bank-match claim.

This is a presentation and evidence improvement. Ledger entries, financial statement values, accounting logic, release tolerances and source-data controls are unchanged.

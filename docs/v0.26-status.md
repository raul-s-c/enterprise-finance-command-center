# v0.26 Status — Invoice-level ECL analysis

## Scope

This release adds a fact-level analytical allocation of the existing
customer-level expected credit-loss (ECL) schedule to open invoices. It applies
the same configured aging rates and risk multiplier already used by the
customer allowance model. It does not change journal entries, allowance
postings, legal net receivables, balance sheet, P&L or cash flow.

The invoice schedule must reconcile gross AR, ECL and net AR by
month/entity/division/customer to the published customer schedule within
EUR 0.05, with no unmatched customers or invoice IDs. Failed controls block
publication. The detail inspector labels this as analytical allocation and
states that the GL provision remains at its existing posting grain.

## Evidence boundary

Invoice source journal IDs and modeled collections/advance applications are
preserved from v0.23. Cash applications are still not bank matched. Invoice
ECL is not a separate journal or invoice-level posted allowance. This release
does not infer payment remittances, customer credit notes, or group
consolidation adjustments.

## Validation

Unit tests cover policy reuse, reconciliation, configured rates, allowance
caps, empty scope, duplicates, invalid balances/buckets and unmatched
customer schedules. The invoice inspector exposes gross open AR, analytical
ECL and analytical net AR at both list and selected-invoice levels, including
a responsive layout for narrow screens.

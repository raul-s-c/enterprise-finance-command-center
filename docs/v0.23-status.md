# v0.23 Status — Invoice-grain receivables evidence

Version 0.23 extends working-capital lineage from customer/month to open
invoice, while preserving the published journal, cash flow and customer-level
AR schedule.

## Reporting contract

- Invoice IDs are the source sale journal IDs; missing or duplicate IDs fail
  the close rather than being reconstructed from customer totals.
- Posted collection credits are analytically allocated to open invoices using
  the existing risk-aware oldest-receivable priority. Customer advance
  applications follow the existing customer-specific oldest-invoice policy.
- Every application retains its source journal ID, month, amount, type and
  allocation basis. It is explicitly modeled and is not bank-matched remittance
  evidence.
- Closing gross open invoice balances reconcile to the published customer
  aging schedule by entity, division, customer and aging bucket. Modeled
  applications reconcile to their source AR credits. No GL, cash or balancing
  entry is created.
- Expected credit-loss allowances remain at customer grain; they are not
  assigned to invoice records.

## Published evidence and experience

The build writes `data/processed/ar_invoice_aging.csv`,
`data/processed/ar_invoice_applications.csv` and a lazy-loaded
`web/data/ar_invoice_detail.json`. The Receivables contribution page exposes a
searchable, paginated invoice view with gross balance, age, source identity and
modeled credit history. Mobile cards and the source trace avoid a wide
invoice-level grid.

## Verification

- Unit controls cover aging reconciliation, source application reconciliation,
  source identity, over-application, empty populations and no mutation of the
  journal input.
- The close continuity test runs two successive offline builds through v0.23,
  preserving action/review history and the 24-month forecast while checking
  invoice controls and matching manifest/dashboard versions.
- The mobile UI regression opens invoice evidence, searches an invoice,
  inspects its source/application trace and checks for horizontal overflow.

## Remaining evidence boundary

Invoice credit allocation is a controlled analytical view, not proof that a
specific customer remitted a specific invoice. Bank statement references,
remittance advice and actual invoice-to-cash matching are not present in the
source model. Inventory is still SKU-grain rather than warehouse-lot grain,
and provisions/consolidation adjustments are not assigned to individual AR
invoices.

# v0.22 Status — 24-month integrated forecast

Version 0.22 extends the rolling scenario forecast from 18 to 24 months. The same configured horizon feeds forecast vintages, workforce planning, liquidity/covenant analysis and integrated P&L, balance sheet and cash flow.

## Reporting contract

- 12-month flow measures sum periods 1–12; 12-month balance measures use the month-12 closing balance.
- 24-month flow measures sum periods 1–24; 24-month balance measures use the month-24 closing balance.
- Minimum liquidity and minimum cash are monitored across the entire 24-month scenario.
- Base, Upside and Downside retain the same complete horizon; controls reject missing or duplicated months and unbalanced statement identities.
- No financial tolerances or accounting balances are changed to enable the longer forecast.

The close-continuity regression builds August and September 2026 successively, checks that previously published action/review history is unchanged, and verifies the new 24-month horizon, statement controls and release metadata.

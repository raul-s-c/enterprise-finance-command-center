# Integrated Three-Statement Forecast

Version 0.13 extends the Enterprise Finance Command Center from a P&L and liquidity outlook into a linked 12-month forecast of the three core financial statements.

The model reuses the same Base, Upside and Downside operating forecast already used by FP&A and Treasury. It does not create a separate set of assumptions for the Balance Sheet or Cash Flow.

## Architecture

```text
Rolling Operating Forecast
        ->
Driver-based Liquidity Forecast
        ->
Forecast P&L
+ Forecast Balance Sheet
+ Forecast Cash Flow
        ->
Hard three-statement controls
```

The forecast state advances once per month and scenario.

## Forecast P&L

The operating forecast supplies:

- Revenue
- Gross Profit before forward asset-quality movements
- OPEX

The integrated statement layer then adds:

- inventory-provision movement
- unrealized intercompany inventory-profit movement
- expected-credit-loss movement
- depreciation
- interest
- tax

The resulting structure is:

```text
Revenue
- Cost of Sales
- Inventory Provision Movement
- Unrealized IC Profit Adjustment
= Gross Profit
- OPEX
- Expected Credit Loss Movement
- Depreciation
= EBIT
- Interest
= EBT
- Tax
= Net Income
```

Book ECL and inventory provisions remain non-deductible in the current synthetic tax policy. The liquidity model therefore calculates tax before those book-only asset-quality movements.

## Forecast Balance Sheet

The Balance Sheet is built from explicit financial drivers rather than from a balancing item.

### Cash

Cash comes directly from the reconciled liquidity forecast.

### Trade receivables

```text
Gross AR forecast
- Expected Credit Loss Allowance
= Net Trade Receivables
```

Gross AR is driven by divisional DSO and customer-prepayment economics.

### Inventory

```text
Gross Inventory forecast
- Inventory Provision
- Unrealized IC Markup Reserve
= Net Consolidated Inventory
```

Gross inventory is driven by physical-division costs and DIO.

### Accounts payable

AP comes from forecast operating accruals and divisional DPO.

### Contract liabilities

Software and Events customer funding is projected independently from Revenue and reduces future cash-conversion requirements.

### PPE and CIP

Future project spend follows the same CAPEX schedules used by the liquidity forecast.

```text
CAPEX Spend
-> CIP
-> Go-live
-> PPE
-> Depreciation
```

No CAPEX balancing assumption is introduced in the three-statement layer.

### Debt

Debt follows scheduled amortization and any RCF draw generated by the liquidity forecast.

### Retained earnings

```text
Opening Retained Earnings
+ Forecast Net Income
= Ending Retained Earnings
```

No balancing plug, forecast equity reserve or unexplained financing line is permitted.

## Forecast Cash Flow

The Cash Flow statement uses the same cash movements already reconciled in the liquidity model.

```text
Operating Cash Flow
- CAPEX
= Free Cash Flow

Free Cash Flow
- Scheduled Debt Repayment
+ RCF Draw
= Net Cash Movement

Opening Cash
+ Net Cash Movement
= Ending Cash
```

Forecast Balance Sheet cash must equal Forecast Cash Flow ending cash for every month and scenario.

## Scenario treatment

The model produces the complete three statements for:

- Base
- Upside
- Downside

Asset-quality reserves are also scenario-sensitive. Downside uses moderately higher reserve assumptions, while Upside uses moderately lower reserve assumptions. The intent is to reflect a deterioration or improvement in asset quality without turning provisions into an arbitrary scenario plug.

## Hard controls

A release is blocked unless all of the following pass:

```text
Forecast Assets - Liabilities - Equity          = 0
Forecast Cash Flow roll-forward                 = 0
Forecast EBIT identity                          = 0
Forecast Net Income identity                    = 0
Forecast BS Cash - Forecast CF Ending Cash      = 0
12 months x 3 scenarios                         = complete
Base scenario                                   = present
Downside scenario                               = present
```

These controls run after all actual-close, Working Capital, provisions, customer-funding, Treasury, Budget, rolling-forecast and liquidity-forecast controls.

## Generated outputs

```text
data/processed/forecast_pnl.csv
data/processed/forecast_balance_sheet.csv
data/processed/forecast_cash_flow.csv
data/processed/three_statement_forecast_summary.csv
```

The CFO application exposes the Base three-statement detail inside the existing P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow views, while Plan & Forecast compares Base, Upside and Downside at scenario level.

## Why this matters

The project can now answer a connected forward-looking question rather than three separate analytical questions:

> If the operating forecast is realized, what happens simultaneously to earnings, cash, assets, liabilities, equity, debt and liquidity?

That is the core purpose of an integrated financial model.

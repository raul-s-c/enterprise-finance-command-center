# Version 0.2 Release Status

Version 0.2 promotes Enterprise Finance Command Center from a finance-engine prototype to a connected synthetic enterprise reporting system.

Implemented in this release:

- six legal entities and four asymmetric divisions
- product and customer master data
- factory capacity and utilization
- product lifecycle and portfolio events
- external and intercompany commercial flows
- 8 percent manufacturing cost-plus transfer pricing
- intercompany settlement and consolidation
- unrealized intercompany inventory-profit elimination
- double-entry accounting
- retained earnings and legal balance sheets
- consolidated P&L, Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
- Working Capital and DSO/DIO/DPO
- CAPEX project portfolio, CIP, PPE and depreciation
- debt, interest and tax mechanics
- product and customer profitability
- price / volume / mix analysis
- 36 rolling months of actuals
- 18-month rolling forecast
- Base, Upside and Downside scenarios
- historical forecast vintages, MAPE and bias
- ECB FX integration with deterministic fallback
- procedural management commentary
- compressed full ledger retention
- expanded release controls
- multi-view CFO GitHub Pages application

Release gate controls cover journal balance, trial balance, legal balance sheets, reciprocal intercompany positions, consolidated balance sheet, cash-flow reconciliation, consolidation revenue/EBIT bridges and forecast look-ahead.

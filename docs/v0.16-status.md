# v0.16 Release Status

Monthly Performance Review and Management Actions release candidate.

Release scope:

- Actual versus Budget P&L review
- Price / Volume / Mix commercial drivers
- reported versus constant-currency FX effects
- month-on-month Working Capital, Free Cash Flow and workforce productivity
- full-year Revenue and EBIT outlook versus Budget
- one-month Revenue forecast accuracy
- Group, Entity, Division and Entity x Division review scopes
- deterministic, source-tied explanations
- owned P1/P2 management action register

Review controls:

- source actual, benchmark and variance tie-out
- unique and current-month review records
- complete required-action coverage
- valid action priority, status, owner and due month
- no orphan management actions
- current-month performance summary

Version 0.16 retains all controls introduced through v0.15, including actual accounting, intercompany, Working Capital, asset quality, customer funding, Treasury, Budget, rolling forecast, workforce planning, functional-currency accounting, EUR translation, forward liquidity and integrated three-statement forecasting.

Final validated counts and control status are written by the production close.
